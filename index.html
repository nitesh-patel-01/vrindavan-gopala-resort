Welcome to Vrindavan Gopala

An Escape Into Nature. A Celebration of Life.

Minutes from Dhuandhar Falls, the resort combines peaceful cottages, lush green surroundings, family experiences and celebrations — in one calm place on the banks of the Narmada at Bhedaghat.

Guests come for the waterfall views, the gardens, and the quiet. Families stay for weddings, birthdays and gatherings that feel personal, not rushed.
