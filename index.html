Why Bhedaghat?

Vrindavan Hotel & Resort, at Bhedaghat in Jabalpur, was established to introduce people to the pristine form of nature. Guests can take part in adventure activities, or simply bask in the warmth of nature — and the resort excels in organising out-of-the-box, unique weddings.

Facilities listed on directories include: in-house restaurant, conference halls, 24-hour room service, laundry and housekeeping, and a reception area for social gatherings.
